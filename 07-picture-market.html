Design 07

The Picture Market ตลาดภาพ

The memory cue is a place. This one is a floating market: chicken, eggs, fish, oranges, mango, water, flowers, and the boat that brought you. Tap a thing. The card opens on that word, the letter inside it, and one reason the tone is what it is.

Learner Someone who remembers a stall more easily than a row in an alphabet chart.
A card holds The object, the word, the letter, a sentence, and the tone in one line.
Another page A symbol with no object of its own waits in the Card Box.

The drawings are flat shapes, enough to judge the walk. A finished market would keep one illustration style and add stalls. Tone marks and vowels appear here when a market word contains them.

  1. 1Tap a thing in the market: the chicken, the mango, the boat.
  2. 2The card opens on that word — its letter, its tone, one sentence.
  3. 3Use “Next stall” to visit every stall in order.
