Design 01

The Card Box กล่องการ์ด

The card is one symbol, and the session is a tray. You see the letter, say what you remember, turn it, and check the class, the sound, the memory word, and one sentence. A card you know leaves the tray. A card you miss goes to the back.

Learner Someone meeting the shapes, or keeping them in a daily tray.
A card holds Symbol, class, sound, picture-word, sentence.
Another page The tone system lives on the Class Board. A continuing day lives in One Morning.

Spaces between Thai words are for learners. Ordinary writing closes them up. The picture is a stand-in: a finished deck would use one drawn style for every memory word. This tray is the first twenty cards, not the whole alphabet.

  1. 1Look at the front. Say the class, the sound, and the memory word.
  2. 2Click the card — or press space — to turn it and check.
  3. 3Got it clears the card from the tray. Again sends it to the back.

The tray is clear.
