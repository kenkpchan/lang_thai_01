One Morning เช้าวันหนึ่ง

The alphabet arrives in the order a Saturday needs it. นิชา and มานพ say good morning, eat, sit in traffic, buy a mango, and get home. Twelve lines. Each line is a card, and the card teaches the one symbol that line is there to carry.

Learner Someone who remembers a sentence more easily than a list.
A card holds The next line, its English, and one symbol from that line.
Another page When the line needs the tone rule, the card names the result and the Class Board keeps the chart.

She uses ค่ะ for a statement and คะ for a question. He uses ครับ. Spaces between words are for learners. Ordinary Thai writing would close them up. This is a weak index of the alphabet and a strong way to keep a sentence.

  1. 1Click a line in the left rail — the conversation runs from 7:10 to noon.
  2. 2Read the line, its English, and the one symbol that line carries.
  3. 3Use “Previous line” and “Next line” to walk the morning in order. Arrow keys work too.
