A frontend for the alphabet

Seven ways to hold a Thai letter. เรียนอักษรไทย

Consonants, vowels, tone marks, and enough words and sentences to make the symbols stick. Each page is a different answer to what a flash card should be. All seven are clickable.

這裡有七個學泰文字母的設計：子音、元音、聲調符號，加上單詞和句子。每一頁都可以點進去試。第一次來，照下面的次序走就可以。

Two of them are specific. 中文助攻 borrows Mandarin, Cantonese, and Teochew. One Morning keeps a single conversation going from card to card.

All seven designs · 七個設計Click a card to open it. 點一張卡，打開那個設計。

  1. 01

    The Card Box กล่องการ์ดOne symbol

    The front is only the letter. The back is its class, its sound, a memory word, and a sentence you could say.

    A tray. Known cards leave. Missed cards go to the back.
  2. 02

    The Class Board แผงวรรณยุกต์The tone rule

    All 44 consonants, sorted into mid, high, and low. Choose a syllable shape and the board names the tone.

    ข and ค are the same puff of air. They are different classes, so the tone can differ.
  3. 03

    The Listening Room ห้องฟังA contrast

    Hear one syllable, then choose the spelling. The lesson is the pair: aspiration, length, tone, or ร beside ล.

    Uses a Thai voice when the computer has one. Otherwise it shows a sound guide.
  4. 04

    中文助攻 The Sinitic Bridge普通话 · 廣東話 · 潮州話

    Every card keeps Mandarin, Cantonese, and Teochew side by side. Borrow a sound when the match is real, and read the warning where Chinese will invent the wrong one.

    Tones stay on the Thai side of the card. The same mark can fall or sit high.
  5. 05

    One Morning เช้าวันหนึ่งA day, one conversation

    Twelve lines, from the door to breakfast, the market, and home. Each line carries one letter, vowel, or tone mark.

    The alphabet arrives in the order the morning needs it.
  6. 06

    The Syllable Bench ม้านั่งพยางค์A syllable you build

    Vowels have seats: before the consonant, above it, below it, after it. Empty seats stay visible. The bench writes the syllable and names the tone.

    When the result is a real word, you get a meaning and a sentence.
  7. 07

    The Picture Market ตลาดภาพA thing in a place

    A canal market. Tap the chicken, the mango, the boat. The word is attached to the object, and the letter comes with the word.

    Spatial memory first. The symbol rides along with the thing you can buy.

The Class Board includes all 44 consonants. The other pages use a starter set so the interaction stays readable. Ordinary syllables follow the tone rule on the board. A silent ห in front of ง ญ น ม ย ร ล ว, pronounced clusters such as ปลา, and irregular spellings such as ก็ and อยู่ are marked where a starter word needs them, and left out of the calculator.
