Design 04 · 中文助攻

中文助攻 The Sinitic Bridge

用普通話、廣東話、潮州話靠近泰語的聲母和詞義。聲調留在泰語這邊，不借用。

This deck is for someone who already speaks Mandarin, Cantonese, or Teochew. The three columns stay side by side, so a Teochew reader is not sent through Mandarin first. Borrow an initial when the match is real. The cards spend their room on those matches, and on the places Chinese will invent the wrong sound: voiced ด and บ, the tap ร, the missing f in Teochew, vowel length, and every tone.

Learner A reader of 中文, 廣東話, or 潮州話.
A card holds The Thai symbol, three bridges, the trap, and one sentence.
Tones The same ้ falls on ป้า and is high on ม้า. That chart is the Class Board.

Column headings are traditional. The Mandarin gloss is simplified. Cantonese and Teochew stay in traditional characters. s, m, n, and w transfer more directly, so they do not take a card. These ten cards are the bridges and the traps.

普通话拼音　b d g 是不送气的清音，不是英语的 b d g。

粤拼　1 阴平，2 阴上，3 阴去，4 阳平，5 阳上，6 阳去。入声并进 1、3、6。

潮拼　b d g 不送气，p t k 送气，bh 才是浊音。调号是潮汕八调，各乡会差一调。只标有把握的词。

  1. 1用「上一张／下一张」或左右方向鍵翻卡。
  2. 2三欄並排：讀你會的那一欄，泰語那行不變。
  3. 3按泰語行的朗讀鍵聽發音。寫著「不要讀成」的地方，就是中文會帶你走的錯路。
