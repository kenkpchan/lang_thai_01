Design 03

The Listening Room ห้องฟัง

Some pairs are easy to see and hard to hear. กา and คา can share a mid tone and still be different sounds. รัก and ลัก share a vowel, a final, and a high tone. The room plays one syllable and offers the spellings it is often confused with.

Learner Someone whose ear collapses aspiration, length, tone, or ร and ล.
A card holds A sound, two or three spellings, and the reason they come apart.
The rule When the answer is a class difference, the Class Board is the chart.

If this computer has a Thai voice, the spelling stays hidden until you answer. If it does not, the room shows a sound guide and the choices stay in Thai. A finished room would use a recorded voice either way. After the answer you can play every choice. The point is the difference.

  1. 1Press Play — or read the sound guide if this computer has no Thai voice.
  2. 2Choose the spelling you heard.
  3. 3Read why the pair comes apart. “Previous pair” and “Next pair” move between drills.

Sound guide, when you see one: unmarked is mid, à is low, â is falling, á is high, ǎ is rising. A doubled vowel is long. g, bp, and dt are unaspirated. kh and ph have a puff of air. ph is not f.
