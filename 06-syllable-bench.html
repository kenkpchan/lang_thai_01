Design 06

The Syllable Bench ม้านั่งพยางค์

A Thai vowel is a seat. เ stands before the consonant. ิ stands above it. ุ stands under it. า stands after it. เ and า can stand on either side at once. This bench keeps those seats visible, including the empty ones.

Learner Someone who can copy a letter and still loses the vowel when it moves.
A card holds The seats, the written syllable, the tone, and a meaning when the word is real.
The rule Live or dead, then the mark. The same chart is the Class Board.

Choose a consonant, a vowel, a final if the vowel allows one, and a mark. The bench writes a real Unicode syllable. If the result is in the starter list, you get the meaning and a sentence. Words such as ก๋ง, บน, and รถ leave the vowel unwritten. This bench writes the vowel you chose.

For the same seats next to the class chart, real words, and Traditional Chinese, open the combined workshop.

  1. 1Choose a consonant on the right.
  2. 2Choose a vowel. Watch which seats it fills: before, above, below, after.
  3. 3Add a final and a tone mark if the vowel allows them.
  4. 4The bench writes the syllable, names the tone, and plays it when a Thai voice exists.

A silent ห in front of ง ญ น ม ย ร ล ว is a later rule, so หนู and ใหม่ are not built here. A pronounced cluster such as ปลา takes the class of the first consonant; this bench uses one initial. ก็ is irregular. Starter words are a sample, so a readable syllable can still be absent from the list.
